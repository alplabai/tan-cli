Why the PREVIOUS recording was incomplete, not wrong
------------------------------------------------------
tan-cli#790 (PR #1022) shipped `sdk remove` reporting WHAT it removed but not
WHAT NOW RESOLVES in its place — deliberately deferred to this issue rather
than folded into that PR (see #1022's own body and #790's tracking issue).
After a force-removal of a load-bearing install (the machine-global default or
a workspace's project pin), the removal envelope gave no signal what resolves
in its place; a caller had to make a separate `tan sdk current` call to find
out, with no way to tell from the removal response alone whether the
workspace falls through to a different tier or has NOTHING left to resolve —
the case a consumer most needs told about.

`resolvesToAfter` answers that by re-running `resolve_sdk_tiered` (the SAME
narrow ladder `remove`'s own load-bearing-removal check already calls) for
the calling workspace, mirroring `sdk current`'s own `data` SHAPE (`sdkPath`,
`readiness`, `sourceTier`) rather than inventing a fourth one — NOT
necessarily the same ANSWER a same-instant `sdk current` call would give:
`sdk current` additionally falls through to a wide discovery walk and honours
an explicit `--sdk-root`, neither of which `resolvesToAfter` does (see
`_resolves_to_after`'s own docstring). It is present on EVERY `sdk remove`
response — idempotent-absent, every refusal, every failure, and a real
removal alike — not only a load-bearing one: an always-present field is
easier for a consumer to code against than a conditional one, and this
hermetic offline-absent case is exactly the shape a workspace with no pin,
no registry entry, and no discoverable checkout reports both before and after
(`sourceTier: "none"`, matching `sdk-current-no-sdk`'s own pinned shape). A
force-removed workspace PROJECT PIN reports this identical `"none"` shape
too, but is a DIFFERENT case this golden does not reach: `.alp/sdk-path` is
left dangling, not cleared, by the removal, so `resolvesToAfter` ALONE
cannot tell that workspace apart from one that was never pinned at all.
tan-cli#1051 closed that gap BESIDE the payload rather than inside it: `sdk
remove` now also carries the `sdk.project-pin-unresolved` warning on
`issues[]` whenever the calling workspace has a pin that no longer resolves,
from the same `project_pin_issue` helper `sdk current` uses. Not necessarily
the byte-identical issue `sdk current` emits: `remove`'s warning names
`resolvesToAfter`'s own narrow tier, `sdk current` names the wide ladder's
(tan-cli#497), so the two can differ by the tier word for one workspace. This golden's workspace has no
`.alp/sdk-path` at all, so its `issues` stays `[]` and the recorded envelope
is unchanged by that fix; the behavioural coverage for it lives in
`test_sdk_command.py`'s dangling-pin cases, which this hermetic golden
cannot reach.

The behavioural cases where it reports something OTHER than
`"none"` — a still-live project pin before removal, and falling through to a
surviving lower tier after a forced one — are covered by
`test_sdk_command.py::test_remove_refuses_the_active_install_without_force_and_force_clears_it`
and
`test_sdk_command.py::test_remove_reports_the_lower_tier_it_falls_through_to_after_a_forced_removal`,
neither of which this hermetic golden can reach (both need a real filesystem
target to remove).
