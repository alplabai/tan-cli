- **`tan renode --sim-mode --timeout 0` can now report `renode.sim-exited-early`
  instead of a blind pass.** The hold loop computed
  `deadline = time.monotonic() + timeout` and then guarded with
  `while time.monotonic() < deadline:` — at `--timeout 0` the deadline was
  already in the past the instant it was read, so the loop body (the only
  place `proc.poll()` was called) never ran even once. `early_exit` stayed
  `None` forever and the `renode.sim-exited-early` arm was structurally
  unreachable at a zero-length hold, regardless of how the child actually
  exited. `_run_sim` now polls the child once up front so a Renode that
  has already exited by the time the hold is reached is still caught, even at
  `--timeout 0`.
