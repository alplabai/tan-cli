- **`tan presets` and `tan clean` now say *why* no SDK resolved, not only that
  none did.** `presets_cmd.resolve_sdk` collapsed to a bare `None` whenever
  nothing usable resolved, dropping `broken_project_pin` and
  `foreign_global_default_for` on the floor even when `resolve_sdk_tiered` had
  already computed them -- so a workspace whose `.alp/sdk-path` names a
  checkout that no longer exists, with no sibling checkout for discovery to
  fall through to and no `~/.alp/sdk-default` either, reported
  `presets.sdk-root-unresolved` / `clean.sdk-root-not-found` alone, with no
  `sdk.project-pin-unresolved` alongside it. `resolve_sdk` now always returns
  an `ActiveSdk` -- `.path is None` is what "nothing usable resolved" looks
  like, distinct from "nothing to report" -- and both callers (plus
  `kconfig`'s own worked-around copy of this gap, `diff`'s text-mode warnings,
  and `pinmux`'s guard against a `Path(None)` crash) were moved from the old
  bare-`None` truthiness check to `.path is not None`. Not the #464
  wrong-checkout harm -- nothing here resolves to a usable SDK at all, so no
  build or clean ever runs against the wrong one -- only the diagnostic gap
  its own docstring had left open.
