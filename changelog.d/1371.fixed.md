- **`tan flash` refuses to write an ITCM-linked image to MRAM.** A Flow D
  (`alif_mram_jlink`) entry, and the SETOOLS `app-gen-toc` auto-sign it runs, now
  read the artefact ELF's LOAD segments before anything spawns and refuse with
  `flash.mram-image-not-mram-linked` when the lowest
  nonzero-FileSiz `p_paddr` is below the SoC's `soc_flash_base`. It is the mirror of
  `flash.ram-image-not-ram-linked` and backs up the manifest's `ram_run_only`
  refusal for a stale or hand-edited manifest. An ELF whose `soc_flash_base`
  cannot be resolved is refused rather than written unchecked.
  The same check covers Flow A (`zephyr_west_flash` with no runner or `runner:
  alif_flash`, which burns MRAM slot0 over the SE-UART), before `west` spawns. The
  two flows differ on one point: Flow D refuses when `soc_flash_base` cannot be
  resolved, while Flow A skips the check, because `zephyr_west_flash` also serves
  non-MRAM boards. An explicit other runner (for example `jlink`) is not checked.
