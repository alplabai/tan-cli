- **Bench follow-ups to the Flow D boot and read-back work (#1458).** The success message names the witness that
  actually confirmed the boot (the PCSR samples and the image range, not DHCSR). When neither witness reads anything
  (a STOP window), `flash.jlink-reset-unconfirmed` is `info` worded "no witness (the target may be in low power)".
  With `--readback` the write session asks for the core to be left halted (`h` before `exit`) and the read-back
  session halts right after `connect`, so the app cannot reach a sleep window between the two. The read-back,
  boot-probe and reset-only sessions each leave a transcript under `flash-logs/`
  (`alif_mram_jlink-<core>-<readback|bootprobe|resettail>-<UTC>.log`, paths in `jlink.readbackSession`,
  `jlink.bootProbe` and `jlink.resetTail`).
