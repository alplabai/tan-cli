- **`MODULE_SIZE_BUDGET_LOG.md`'s append-only claim is now enforced.** The
  ledger's own header said every entry only ever appends at the end, but
  nothing checked it -- a merge conflict resolved with `git checkout --theirs`
  silently dropped a branch's own reasoned entry (tan-cli#902), and a
  hand-written entry once landed above the file's existing tail instead of
  after it (tan-cli#878), and both slipped through every gate that ran that
  day. `python/tests/gates/test_ledger_append_only.py` now asserts the
  ledger's content at `merge-base(HEAD, dev)` is an exact prefix of the
  working file; when HEAD is (part of) a merge, each parent's own unique
  tail must additionally survive as an order-preserving subsequence of
  whatever follows that merge's common ancestor, which is what lets a
  legitimate "keep both sides" resolution interleave two branches' entries
  by date and still pass. Degrades to a loud `pytest.skip` (never a silent
  pass) on a detached checkout with no `dev` ref to compare against.
