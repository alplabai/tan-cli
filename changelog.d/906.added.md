- **`MODULE_SIZE_BUDGET_LOG.md`'s append-only claim is now checked, in CI and
  locally.** The ledger's own header said every entry only ever appends at
  the end, but nothing checked it -- a merge conflict resolved with
  `git checkout --theirs` silently dropped a branch's own reasoned entry
  (tan-cli#902), and a hand-written entry once landed above the file's
  existing tail instead of after it (tan-cli#878), and both slipped through
  every gate that ran that day.
  `python/tests/gates/test_ledger_append_only.py` asserts the ledger's
  content at `merge-base(HEAD, dev)` is an exact prefix of the working file;
  when HEAD is, or has, a merge commit anywhere between that base and HEAD
  (not only when HEAD is the merge itself -- the ordinary "merge, keep
  working, push" shape), each parent's own unique tail must additionally
  survive as an order-preserving subsequence of whatever follows that
  merge's common ancestor, which is what lets a legitimate "keep both sides"
  resolution interleave two branches' entries by date and still pass.
  `ci.yml`'s `python` job and `parity.yml`'s `seam1-plan-shape` job now fetch
  full history (`fetch-depth: 0`) so the gate can actually resolve `dev`
  against the shallow PR checkout both jobs otherwise get; in CI
  (`GITHUB_ACTIONS` set) an undeterminable base is a hard failure rather than
  a skip, since a skip is invisible in the CI summary this gate's whole
  premise is not to be. Locally, off CI, it still degrades to a loud
  `pytest.skip` naming what it tried, never a silent pass.
