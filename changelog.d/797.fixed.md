- **`tan doctor` no longer risks running a project-local `git`/`py`/`python`
  in place of the real one.** Five spawns in `doctor_cmd.py` -- the SDK-
  provenance `git` probes (`_is_own_git_checkout`, `_git_short_commit`,
  `_git_behind_upstream`), the raw `git config --get core.longpaths` read, and
  the host-Python probe's `py`/`python`/`python3` candidates -- handed
  `subprocess` a bare identity rather than the PATH-resolved absolute path.
  On Windows, `CreateProcess` searches the current directory -- normally the
  customer's project -- BEFORE `%PATH%`, so a checked-in or unpacked
  `git.exe`/`py.exe`/`python.exe` ran with `tan`'s privileges during what
  users reasonably believe is a read-only diagnostic, and could silently
  corrupt `sdkProvenance`'s reported commit. All five now resolve through
  `tan.core.tool_lookup.resolve_tool` and spawn with `executable=` pinned,
  keeping the child's own bare `argv[0]` for its diagnostics; when the tool
  is not on PATH at all, the affected checks degrade to "no answer" rather
  than crashing `tan doctor`.
