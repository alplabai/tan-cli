- **`tan flash` (Flow D) confirms a healthy flash of a sleeping app again, via the kernel uptime.**
  When the post-write halt fails and `DHCSR` `S_RESET_ST` is gone, the existing non-halting boot-probe session now also reads Zephyr's `curr_tick` four times, 600 ms apart, at the address the flashed ELF gives it (never hardcoded; DTCM / SRAM0 of the slice core only, never HP ITCM).
  A tick count that rises and ends at 30 s or less proves the new image booted within seconds, which the replaced image cannot fake: `jlink.reset: "pin-reset"`, `jlink.resetConfirmedBy: "uptime"`, evidence in `jlink.bootProbe.uptime`, and no `flash.jlink-reset-unconfirmed`.
  A missing, duplicated or unreadable symbol, a large or flat counter, or an unknown tick rate (`CONFIG_SYS_CLOCK_TICKS_PER_SEC` from the ELF, else the `.config` beside it) keeps today's honest `unconfirmed`; nothing new can fail the flash (tan-cli#1539).
