- **`tan flash` refuses a `yocto_wic` target that is mounted, used as swap, or backs a mounted filesystem.** A mistyped `flash_args.target` (`/dev/sda`) passed the block-device check and `dd` overwrote the workstation's root disk. The write now reads `/proc/self/mountinfo` (maj:min resolved through `/sys/dev/block`), `/proc/swaps` and the partition/device-mapper parents, and refuses with the new `flash.yocto-wic-target-in-use`; when the mount table or sysfs cannot be read it refuses too, since the target cannot be proven unused. Ported from alp-sdk#3085.
